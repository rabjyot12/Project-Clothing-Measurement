Garment Vision

Calibrated, manually verified clothing measurements · Validation against manual ground truth

1. Garment & photo

Place the garment flat on a contrasting plain surface. Keep the camera directly above it. Place a ruler in the same plane as the garment, near the area being measured. A tilted camera can cause errors even with correct software.

2. Calibration

Tap exactly two points on the ruler, 15 cm apart if value is 15. Do not select ruler edges ambiguously.

3. Select points

Choose an image first.

Blue: calibration · Red: garment measurement · Green: suggested boundary. Tap twice per segment. Pinch zoom is disabled on image; use browser zoom or a sharper photo.

4. Manual reference (optional)

Enter independently measured flat-garment widths/lengths, in cm. These are needed to calculate MAE. Do not enter approximate values and claim verified accuracy.
